        About seller and buyer passwords

        Nobody can see a seller's or buyer's password, including you. Firebase keeps passwords only in scrambled (hashed) form inside Authentication, not in the Firestore database, so a leaked database never exposes them.

        To change a password: click Send password reset next to the seller or buyer above. They get an email with a link to choose a new password. People can also use Forgot password? on the seller and shop sign-in pages.

        To see every account: Firebase console → Authentication → Users shows each email, when they signed up and when they last signed in. Its ⋮ menu can also disable or delete an account.
